# Numbers & Handover

Numerical claims and project handovers must be reproducible.

1. Derive counts, ratios and durations from original test logs or machine-readable artifacts; never transcribe them from memory or an earlier summary.
2. Validate expected fields, revision and totals. If the source is missing, mark the number UNVERIFIED.
3. Verify the final document by reading it back; do not assume updating its source updated an exported PDF.
4. Handover must state date, repository/branch/revision, uncommitted and unpushed work, running jobs, tests actually run, blockers, risks and next step.
5. An old test result remains historical even if it is still the newest available. Date it; don't call it current verification.
6. Preserve historical research records and append dated corrections rather than silently rewriting them.
7. Use the smallest useful inspection and keep reports concise. An estimate without evidence is an unverified estimate.
